## Original artwork (created for this site)

These were drawn for this project and are not derived from any template:

- `hero/hero-sky.png` — gradient sky + clouds, Warm Sand & Pine palette
- `hero/hero-hills.webp` — rolling hills band (transparent above the ridge),
  re-tinted from this repo's own `SkyHills` SVG in
  `src/components/illustrations.tsx`
- `hero/hero-dashboard.webp` — dashboard mockup matching the site's own
  `HeroDashboard` component
- `hero/hero-particles.svg` — white wave band under the hero
- `src/app/icon.svg` — favicon/sun mark
